About

MediAssist AI

A Smart Disease Prediction & Patient Support System

MediAssist AI is an educational healthcare application designed to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can support health-related decision making through an interactive and user-friendly platform. Users can describe symptoms, analyse skin condition images, and provide feedback to receive AI-generated insights. The application aims to promote health awareness and showcase the potential of AI in healthcare while serving as an educational prototype. It is intended for demonstration purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
